## The November 1974 Murders

On the night of November 13, 1974, 23-year-old Ronald DeFeo Jr. systematically executed six members of his family inside their Dutch Colonial house at 112 Ocean Avenue. All six victims—his parents and four siblings—were discovered face down in their beds, each slain with a .35-caliber Marlin rifle.

What baffled investigators immediately was the lack of struggle or auditory disturbance. Despite the rifle lacking a suppressor, neighbors reported hearing no gunshots, and toxicology reports confirmed none of the victims were drugged. DeFeo later claimed in court testimony that "voices from the house" commanded him to kill.

## The 28-Day Lutz Occupancy

Thirteen months after the tragedy, George and Kathleen Lutz purchased the waterfront property for $80,000. Twenty-eight days later, they fled the house, abandoning their furniture, clothing, and personal belongings.

Their claims included:
* Unexplained cold spots dropping below 40°F in sealed rooms.
* Green gelatinous exudate oozing from carpet fibers and locks.
* Demonic visual manifestations, including a red-eyed porcine creature hovering outside a second-story window.
* Inverted crucifixes and physical levitation during nocturnal hours.

Phenomenologists Ed and Lorraine Warren conducted an infrared photographic session inside the home in 1976, capturing the controversial image of a young boy looking out from a doorway—alleged to be John DeFeo's spirit.
