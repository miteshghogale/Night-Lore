## The Uintah Basin Hotbed

Stretching across 512 acres of remote Utah desert, Skinwalker Ranch (formerly Sherman Ranch) sits at the border of the Ute Indian Reservation. For generations, tribal history warned against entering the basin, identifying it as the path of the *Skinwalker*—a shapeshifting entity in Navajo folklore.

In 1994, Terry and Gwen Sherman purchased the ranch to raise cattle. Within two years, they observed unexplainable craft hovering over the ridges, multi-ton calves vanishing from padlocked enclosures, and bullet-proof canine entities attacking livestock.

## Defense Intelligence Agency & NIDS Studies

In 1996, real estate mogul Robert Bigelow acquired the property and founded NIDS (National Institute for Discovery Science), assembling astrophysicists, bio-physicists, and former intelligence personnel.

Under the DIA's AAWSAP program (Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program), contract teams documented:
1. **Exotic Cattle Mutilations**: Surgical incisions executed with laser-like precision, void of tissue carbonization or predator tracks, drained entirely of blood.
2. **Sub-surface Transient Craft**: Thermal imaging capturing subterranean thermal plumes moving at speeds exceeding 200 mph.
3. **RF Spectrum Interference**: Persistent high-powered radio frequency emissions at 1.6 GHz prior to anomalous physical manifestations.
