In 1949, a Jesuit priest kept a private diary of an exorcism so disturbing... it inspired "The Exorcist."

A teenage boy in Maryland had begun exhibiting violent behavior... speaking in altered voices... and displaying strange phenomena that adult witnesses could not explain.

Priests performed a series of solemn exorcism rites over several agonizing weeks. They documented every session in a private diary... a record that stayed sealed from the public for decades.

The film that followed changed horror forever. But the real, primary logs tell a very different story from the movie screen.
