Nine experienced hikers pitched a tent in 1959.

When investigators found it... the heavy canvas had been slashed open... from the inside.

The group had fled out into a minus thirty degree Celsius blizzard... wearing only socks. No boots. No coats.

Search teams found five of the hikers dead of hypothermia. But two months later... they uncovered the other four. Buried deep in a ravine.

Their bodies showed crushed ribs and a fractured skull... yet there was no bruising. No external wounds anywhere.

The Soviet prosecutor abruptly closed the case... citing "an unknown compelling force."

For sixty years... no one could explain those horrific internal injuries.

Then, in 2021, scientists finally ran the physics... and what they discovered was almost worse than a monster.
