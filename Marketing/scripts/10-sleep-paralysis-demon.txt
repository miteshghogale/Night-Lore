You wake up. You can't move.

And something... is standing at the foot of your bed.

It is called sleep paralysis. It happens when your brain wakes up before your body does. You are trapped in REM atonia... the muscle paralysis that normally prevents you from acting out your dreams.

But almost every culture on Earth describes the exact same terrifying detail: a dark, watching figure in the bedroom.

The "Old Hag" in Europe. "Kanashibari" in Japan. A demon sitting heavily on the chest in dozens of other traditions.

Different cultures. Different centuries. The exact same shadow.

Neuroscience has a theory... but science still hasn't fully explained why that dark figure is always there.
