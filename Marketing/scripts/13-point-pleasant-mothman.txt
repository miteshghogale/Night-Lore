For thirteen months, a small West Virginia town reported a winged, red-eyed creature.

Then... a bridge fell into the river.

Starting in 1966, residents of Point Pleasant described a tall, winged humanoid with glowing red eyes... perching near an old munitions site.

Sightings kept coming for over a year. Police officers, young couples, factory workers... all describing the exact same entity.

Then, on December 15, 1967... the Silver Bridge suddenly collapsed during rush-hour traffic... killing forty-six people.

The creature sightings stopped almost entirely after that catastrophic night.

To this day, no one has ever explained why those two events lined up so precisely.
