At the base of Mount Fuji... lies a forest so dense that compasses stop working inside it.

Aokigahara's soil is packed with volcanic magnetite... throwing off magnetic needles and making the woods deeply disorienting to navigate.

The trees grow so tightly together that almost no wind ever reaches the forest floor. Locals call it the "Sea of Trees"... named for the total, heavy silence inside.

For centuries, Japanese folklore has held that these trees are home to yūrei... restless spirits who never found peace.

Scientists and folklorists still disagree... on whether the unnatural silence itself is what unsettles travelers... or if something far older lingers beneath the canopy.
