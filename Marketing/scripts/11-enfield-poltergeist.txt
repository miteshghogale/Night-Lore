For eighteen months, a London council house was investigated for levitating furniture... and a voice that wasn't the little girl's.

Enfield, North London, late 1970s. The Hodgson family reported heavy chairs moving on their own... objects flying across rooms... and their young daughter allegedly levitating right in front of witnesses.

Investigators from the Society for Psychical Research spent months recording inside the residence. They captured a harsh, guttural voice... one that did not match anything a child should be able to produce.

Police officers were called to the house. Neighbors watched phenomena happen in broad daylight.

Decades later... researchers still cannot agree on what those primary audio recordings actually prove.
