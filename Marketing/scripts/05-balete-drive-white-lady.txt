A taxi driver picked up a woman in a white nightgown.

When he checked his mirror... the back seat was empty.

It was Quezon City, Philippines, 1953. The woman had entered the cab without a sound. She asked for a destination in a hollow whisper... and then vanished mid-drive. The doors remained locked.

When the driver looked up into his rearview mirror... a bruised, bloodied face was staring directly back at him.

Within weeks, taxi drivers across Manila were reporting the exact same passenger... along the exact same dark, tree-lined road.

To this day... some Manila cab drivers still refuse to take fares down Balete Drive after midnight.
