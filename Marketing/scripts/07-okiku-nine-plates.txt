Every night... from the deep, dark bottom of a well... something counts to nine.

And then... it stops.

Okiku was a servant girl blamed for losing one of ten precious plates. Depending on the version of the legend... she was murdered, or took her own life, and her body was thrown into the family well.

Ever since that tragic night... witnesses claim her ghost rises from the dark water to count the plates.

One... two... three... all the way to nine.

Then... total silence.

Then... a blood-curdling scream echoing through the dark.

The tenth plate is never found. And beneath the cold stone... she never stops counting.
