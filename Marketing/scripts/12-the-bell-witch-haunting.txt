Legend says even Andrew Jackson refused to spend a second night on this remote Tennessee farm.

It all started in 1817... when farmer John Bell shot at a strange animal in his cornfield.

Within days, his family was being systematically tormented. Loud gnawing inside the wooden walls. Invisible hands striking his daughter in front of witnesses.

And eventually... an unseen voice that could recite Scripture from memory... and knew local gossip no stranger could possibly know.

The entity called itself Kate... and it declared a deadly hatred for John Bell by name.

In 1820... the voice openly bragged about poisoning his medicine.

John Bell was dead the next morning.
