In December 1975, a family moved into a house where six people had been murdered just one year earlier.

They lasted exactly twenty-eight days.

George and Kathy Lutz bought 112 Ocean Avenue for a steal... because thirteen months prior, Ronald DeFeo Jr. had shot his entire family in their beds while they slept. None of them ever woke.

George and Kathy moved in anyway.

Within weeks, the horror began. Unexplained cold spots throughout the rooms. Green slime exuding on the walls. And night after night... something began waking them... at the exact hour of the murders.

Then came day twenty-eight.

The family fled. They left in complete panic... carrying nothing but the clothes on their backs. They never returned for their belongings.

The forensic ballistics. The court history. The full DeFeo case file. It is all waiting for you on Night Lore.
