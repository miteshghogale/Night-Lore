Two kids knocked on a journalist's car window and asked for a ride.

Then... he saw their eyes.

Abilene, Texas, 1996. A local reporter was sitting in his parked car late at night... when two boys in hoodies appeared out of nowhere.

They asked to be let inside... insisting, again and again, that they could not enter unless he opened the door himself.

When he finally looked directly at their faces beneath the streetlight... his blood ran cold.

Their eyes were completely black. No white. No pupil. Just solid, empty dark.

He slammed the pedal and sped away in terror.

When he checked his rearview mirror seconds later... the empty parking lot had nothing in it. No kids. Nowhere to run.
