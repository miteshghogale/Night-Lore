A farmer found footprints in the deep snow... leading straight to his remote house.

None leading away.

Days later, someone slaughtered his entire family of six... with a mattock in a Bavarian barn.

And then... the killer stayed inside.

For four long days, neighbors watched smoke rise steadily from the chimney. Inside the silent house, the killer fed the family's cattle. Milked them on schedule. Baked fresh bread in the family's oven.

Money and gold were later found completely untouched in unlocked drawers. Robbery was not the motive.

Over one hundred years later, this case remains completely unsolved... and the unknown killer was never seen leaving that property.
