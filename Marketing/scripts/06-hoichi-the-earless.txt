A blind musician performed for a royal court every night for six days.

He didn't know he was singing to the dead.

Hōichi was a blind biwa player at a Japanese temple, famous for reciting the tragic fall of the Heike clan.

Each night, a mysterious warrior led him away into the darkness to perform. Hōichi returned every morning, exhausted and pale.

Spotted by worried priests, they followed him into the night... and found him playing alone in a dark graveyard. Surrounded by ghostly flames. Applauded by skeletal hands.

To hide him from the spirits, the priests painted sacred text over every inch of his body.

They forgot one part.

That night... the ghosts came back... for exactly what was left uncovered.
